Coldly — your next clients, contacted for you.

Coldly finds real businesses that match who you want to reach, for example "web agencies in Birmingham", using Google Maps and each company's own website.

For every business, Coldly writes a personal cold email based on real details about that company, sends it from your own email account, and follows up automatically if there's no reply.

When someone replies, Coldly notifies you on WhatsApp with a link that opens the conversation directly in Gmail, so you can take it from there.

All leads are delivered to your own Google Sheet, together with the exact emails Coldly sends.
