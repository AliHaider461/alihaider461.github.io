Privacy Policy — Coldly

Last updated: 17 November 2026 · Home

Who we are

Coldly is a lead generation and email outreach tool. Contact: haider3.claude@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address when you use Coldly.
  • Business contact information: publicly available business details such as company name, website, business email addresses and phone numbers, collected from Google Maps listings and companies' public websites.
  • Google account data: with your permission, Coldly connects to your Google account to write leads into your Google Sheets, send emails from your Gmail account, and detect replies to those emails.

How we use information

We use it only to provide Coldly's service: finding leads, writing and sending outreach emails on your behalf, tracking replies, and notifying you. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google user data

Coldly's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We access only the Google data needed for the features above and do not share it with third parties except service providers needed to run Coldly.

Storage and security

Data is stored with trusted infrastructure providers and protected with access controls. We keep data only as long as needed to provide the service, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

You can disconnect Coldly from your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and ask us to delete your data by emailing haider3.claude@gmail.com.

Businesses we contact

If you received an email sent through Coldly and don't want further messages, reply "no" or "unsubscribe". Your address will be added to a permanent do-not-contact list.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
